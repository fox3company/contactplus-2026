    Petya Stoykova is the founder, choreographer and teacher of DUNE Dance Company and Dance School in Burgas. Her work moves between contemporary dance creation, education and projects that develop the local dance scene.

Alongside her work with DUNE, she teaches contemporary dance in Burgas and has been a guest teacher in international projects in Bulgaria, Moldova and Ukraine. Her choreography has received the Margarita Arnaudova Award for Choreography.
